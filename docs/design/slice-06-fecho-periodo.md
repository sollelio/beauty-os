## 1. Scope

Designed: the **Fecho** space for the current period on a smartphone, inside the authorized/private context: the period home (what still needs attention), the exception list, the compact professional review with its determinate, overdrawn and pending cases, the decision flow for a contextual rule (and its change while the period is open), the period's money review (expenses, purchases, reserve), the operational position, the owners' distribution decision, the approval boundary, the payment-ready view for finance, the payment-in-progress and partial-payment states, closing, the closed read-only state, authorized reopening, the decision history, the error and edge states, and one explicitly open candidate (cash check).

Not designed: accounting, payroll, tax, reporting, owner accounting, Hoje (only its existing handoff), Equipa (Slice 04 is reused by handoff), Dinheiro and Stock spaces, the authorization mechanism, period configuration, reserve-allocation capture, correction of a confirmed payment or of any recorded event, desktop layouts, notifications, deletion of anything.

## 2. The close, as designed

**Fact** (01 §1): today the period is reconstructed from notebooks, totals, advances, cash and WhatsApp. **Decided** (02 §4): the product moves from *reconstruct the month* to *capture once → calculate continuously → flag exceptions → review → decide*. **Decided** (03 §2 · Period states, 04 J5 — recorded in the correction pass): the period moves through `Open (Aberto) → Ready for payment (Pronto para pagamento) → Payment in progress (Em pagamento) → Closed (Fechado)`; confirmed payments may exist before *Closed*; a period cannot become *Closed* while approved payable amounts remain unpaid; closed periods are not casually editable and reopening is traced. **Decided** (04 J5 — recorded in the final semantic correction): remaining payable per person = `max(earned − advances − confirmed payments, 0)`, never negative; an advance above earned value is neither earned remuneration, debt nor credit, and stays a separate management case whose policy is open (03 §5). **Hypothesis** (04 J5 journey steps): the review runs *review → resolve exceptions → approve amounts → pay → confirm payments → close*. Still open (03 §5): partial payments and overpayments, when the first payment becomes authorized, payment correction, the state a reopened period returns to.

Fecho computes nothing new. Every number on its screens is a sum of records captured during the period (services, advances, expenses, purchases with contributions — Slices 01–05) plus the decisions recorded in the close itself. The one exception is the **reserve allocation**: canon requires it as a money movement distinct from an expense (03 §2 #8, 04 J3), but no slice has designed its capture; Fecho shows it as a record captured elsewhere (§12 Q10). What Fecho adds is **decisions** and **confirmations**, each recorded with who and when:

| Record made in Fecho | Basis | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Regra do período (contextual rule set, or changed, for a person and period) | 03 §3 `Decided`; 01 §2; change-while-open: **New product decision** | The person's earned and payable values start to exist; the period position completes. |
| Decisão dos sócios (sem distribuição / um valor) as an explicit record | 01 §3 `Confirmed` that the decision exists; recording it explicitly rather than as a generic money movement: **New product decision** (03 §5 open) | Shown as "Não distribuído"; never derived; never forced. |
| Valores a pagar aprovados (period-level, all people at once) | 04 J5 step 7; period-level and all-at-once: **New product decision** | Period → *Pronto para pagamento*. |
| Pagamento confirmado (per person: value, method, date, who) | 04 J5 steps 8–9; Slice 04 §3 | Period → *Em pagamento*; appears in the person's situation. |
| Período fechado / reaberto (reason mandatory) | 04 J5 `Decided` traceability and close blocked while approved amounts are unpaid; mandatory reason: **New product decision** | Read-only; reopening traced. |

UI labels for the states: **Aberto · Pronto para pagamento · Em pagamento · Fechado**. "Outubro" and all dates are sample data; the period is not assumed to be a calendar month (03 §5).

Nothing is recorded until *Confirmação necessária* (Slice 02 boundary). |

This keeps the loop in §6 reachable. **New product decision**: a rule change is a new record, allowed until approval. |
| **No universal percentage, no named rule** | The field is a percentage for this person and this period; chips come from that person's history. Fixed-rule people never see this flow. Other rule shapes (03 §2 #6) are configuration and out of scope. |

Board D1 draws the cycle. |
| **Business position without accounting** (03 §2 #12; 01 §3) | One headline, **Livre para decisão dos sócios** = produção − ganhos da equipa − despesas − compras (parte do salão) − reserva alocada − entregue acima do ganho, with its derivation and a numbered explanation sheet. *Ganhos da equipa* is what the team earned under each person's rule (170.000), never more. While a rule is pending the headline is "—" and the explanation gives the honest partial, always under the one name **Sobra · antes das regras por definir**. The word *lucro* appears only as a negation. |
| **Money concepts stay distinct** (01 §2 `Decided`; CLAUDE.md #10) | One phrase per concept: *ganhos da equipa*; *adiantamentos* and *pagamentos* up to the earned value (ways of delivering earnings, not costs); *entregue acima do ganho* (money that left, not earned, not debt, not credit, policy open); *despesas*; *compras (salão)* vs *contribuições* (à parte); *reserva alocada* ("dinheiro do salão · não distribuível", never an expense); *distribuição aos sócios* (a decision, not a salary); **Não distribuído** for livre − distribuição. "Fica no salão" is not used, because it would name the reserve, the undistributed remainder and a retained amount alike. |
| **Over-advanced professional: payable clamps at 0; the excess is its own case** (04 J5 `Decided`, final semantic correction; the policy for the excess stays `Open`, 03 §5) | Per person, remaining payable = `max(earned − advances − confirmed payments, 0)`. Laurindo: earned 29.000, advances 35.000 → payable 0; his earned value stays 29.000 and the team's earnings stay 170.000. The 6.000 delivered above earned is shown separately everywhere: the home item *Acima do ganho · A rever*, the review sheet F4b ("Ganhou menos do que recebeu em adiantamentos: 6.000 Kz entregues acima do ganho … não fica registado como ganho, dívida nem crédito; o que fazer com ele é decisão da gestão, ainda em aberto"), its own row in the position, its own step in the explanation, and the approval boundary. The obligations row reads *A pagar à equipa 106.000 = ganhos 170.000 − adiantamentos até ao ganho 64.000 − pagos 0*, which equals the sum of the clamped per-person values. No recovery, carry-over or write-off is implied. |
| **Expenses, purchases, reserves: concise, not a ledger** (brief; 05 §1 Dinheiro) | *Dinheiro do período*: group totals with the few rows behind them, "Ver em Dinheiro" in the group header, the purchase split into salon part and contributions, a reserve card (allocated this period; "dinheiro do salão: não é despesa nem é distribuível; o registo da alocação é capturado fora do Fecho"), and the team movements (advances, payments) as links. No running reserve balance is shown: nothing captures its inflows and outflows (§12 Q10). |
| **Finance handoff replaces WhatsApp totals** (01 §1; 04 J5 step 8) | *Pagamentos* is the consolidated view for whoever processes payments, possibly remotely: approved-by line, three totals (Aprovado · Pago · Por pagar), one row per person with the approved amount **and its derivation**, and a state pill (*Por pagar* · *Pago* with value, method, date, who · *Nada a pagar* with the reason · *Parcial*). | **A payment may exist before Closed** (04 J5; Slice 04 §3) | The first confirmed payment moves the period to *Em pagamento*; the home then says "2 de 3 pagamentos confirmados · falta 39.000". Approved, paid and unpaid are shown as three numbers, never collapsed. |

After it, amounts change only by **anulando a aprovação** (possible while no payment exists; traced). | **Closed = reliable record** (04 J5 `Decided`) | Closed home: lock card ("Fechado em 3 nov às 11:45 por Mercy"), read-only summary whose rows add up to *Não distribuído*, section links, one tertiary *Reabrir período (autorização necessária)*. | **Private context, no mechanism** (05 §3–§4) | Every Fecho screen, including the rule flow and every boundary, carries the *Privado* badge (Slice 04 pattern). No PIN, role or login UI. |

## 6. Contextual remuneration and the owners' decision — the cycle without hidden circularity

**Fact** (01 §2–§3): some people's rate is decided per period by the owners; owners decide at period end whether a distribution is affordable; in the pilot the person with the contextual rate is also an owner, so one decision can move the other. **Decided** (03 §3, 05 §7): no provisional value is ever final. **Open** (04 J5): the rule for resolving the circularity.

The design does not resolve the business rule. It makes the loop explicit, reachable and harmless:

1. **Rever posição**: every defined rule counts; pending ones are named; the headline is "—" and the explanation gives *Sobra · antes das regras por definir* (131.000 = 300.000 − ganhos definidos 99.000 − 23.500 − 20.500 − 20.000 − 6.000 acima do ganho, before both rules; 99.000 after Carla's). The rule screen previews the period figure as base − the new earned value − any new excess (Carla at 10%: 121.000 = 131.000 − 6.400 − 3.600 acima do ganho); changing an existing rule first takes that person's current earned value and excess out of the base (Carla 70% → 50%: 60.000 = 92.000 − 32.000).
2. **Definir a regra pendente**: the person's screen shows, for the typed percentage, the effect on the person and on the period, each with its derivation. For an owner-professional, a notice says the two decisions are separate, and a line shows a distribution already recorded and what would remain undistributed; a negative remainder is shown signed and warned.
3. **Rever o livre para os sócios**: with every rule defined the headline becomes a number with its derivation, including any amount delivered above earned as its own term.
4. **Decidir a distribuição**: *Sem distribuição* or an amount; "Não distribuído" is shown; a value above the free amount warns (does not block); the decision is recorded with who and when. It can be recorded while a rule is still pending; then its effect shows "—" until the last rule is set (the sample timeline does exactly this: Carla 18:12 → distribution 18:15 → Fernando 18:20).
5. **Aprovar valores**: only with every rule defined. The loop 4 → 2 is open while the period is *Aberto*: from a person's review sheet, *Alterar regra do período* reopens the rule flow with the current rule stated; each change is a new record ("Regra alterada · de 50% para 40%"). After approval, a rule changes only by annulling the approval, and not at all once a payment is confirmed; the approval boundary says so when it applies.

Nothing is computed from the other decision; the product only shows consequences, including negative ones. **New product decision** (the design's stance, for 04 J5 to take explicitly): two independent records, no derivation between them, any order, loop open until approval, negative consequences warned but not blocked. **Ambiguity recorded for product policy** (§12): who may set a rule and who may record the owners' decision; whether the owners' decision must exist before closing (designed: warn only).

| Compras (salão) | purchase totals minus the contributions of people | Slice 03 (sum of contributors = total) |

| Distribuição aos sócios | the owners' recorded decision | Fecho |
| Não distribuído | livre − distribuição; signed and warned when negative | derived |
| A pagar à equipa | Σ per person max(earned − advances − confirmed payments, 0); shown as ganhos − adiantamentos até ao ganho (Σ min(advances, earned)) − pagos, which is the same sum; in the sample 170.000 − 64.000 − 0 = 106.000 (0 + 45.000 + 22.000 + 39.000), and 170.000 − 64.000 − 45.000 = 61.000 after Nádia's payment | 04 J5 `Decided` clamp; derived |

Both lenses are explainable step by step. *Lucro* is never used as a label (01 §3).

## 8. Payments and the finance handoff

- Approval is one period-level boundary; it records who approved, when, the total and the cases it acknowledged, and moves the period to *Pronto para pagamento*.
- *Pagamentos* shows **who needs payment, how much, from what (derivation), what is pending, what has been paid**. Before approval it reads "A pagar · por aprovar" and nothing can be confirmed.
- A confirmed payment records value, method, date and who confirmed; it appears in the person's situation (Slice 04) and moves the period to *Em pagamento*.
- The amount is prefilled with the approved remainder. A lower amount **warns and records** (E2, with *Repor* to restore the approved value); the row becomes *Parcial*; the period cannot close until the remainder is confirmed. A higher amount is blocked, as the enforcement of "approved amounts change only with a record"; **New product decision** either way (§12 Q2).
- No messaging or WhatsApp integration; the view is the handoff.

## 9. Closed period, reopening, audit

- *Fechado*: read-only by default, lock card with who/when, a summary whose rows add up, links into the sections, no primary action. Period sheets elsewhere (Slice 04 V3) already say "só se alteram por reabertura autorizada no Fecho".
- *Reabrir*: an authorization boundary with a mandatory reason; what was approved, paid and decided stays recorded; the period returns to *Em pagamento* when payments exist, otherwise *Pronto para pagamento*. In this slice reopening allows the owners' decision to be revised and unconfirmed payments to be confirmed, then closing again (the screen says so); correcting a confirmed payment, a rule or the approval after payment is not designed (§12 Q2, Q6).
- *Decisões e histórico*: the close's own decisions only, newest first, with who and when. Operational events stay in their spaces' histories (Slice 04 S3, Dinheiro).

## 11. Privacy handling

Everything in Fecho is sensitive financial information (05 §4) and sits behind the authorization boundary, marked *Privado* on every screen. Nothing changes what the shared-device flows reveal. Fecho shows attributions ("definida por Mercy", "aprovado por Fernando", "confirmado por Mercy"); whether every authorized viewer may see all attributions is part of the open authorization model (03 §5).

## 12. Open questions (`Open / requires validation`) and new product decisions

1. **Who decides what.** Who may set or change a contextual rule, record the owners' decision, approve, confirm payments, close and reopen (03 §5 authorization). The sample attributes rules, the owners' decision and payments to Mercy and the approval to Fernando; nothing depends on that split.
2. **Partial and over payments** — `Decided` as proposed ([07 D7](../07_PRE_IMPLEMENTATION_GAP_CLOSURE.md#d7--close-rules-payments-approval-closing-records-after-approval-reopen)): a lower amount warns and records (*Parcial*), a higher amount is blocked. Alternatives: lock the amount to the approved value; allow any amount with a warning. Related and not designed: how a wrong confirmed payment is corrected (reopen vs correction record).
3. **Changing a rule after approval** — `Decided` as proposed (07 D7): only by annulling the approval, before any payment; new records in an approved period are rejected. Alternative: per-person approval so that one disputed person does not hold the others' payments.
4. **Closing with unpaid amounts** — `Decided` (04 J5, this pass): a period cannot become *Fechado* while approved payable amounts remain unpaid; the close boundary is blocked with the reason (E1).
5. **Closing without an owners' decision** — `Decided` as proposed (07 D7): warn only ("fica registado «sem decisão»").
6. **Reopen target state** — `Decided` (07 D7): back to *Em pagamento* when payments exist, otherwise *Pronto para pagamento*; the post-approval operations become available again (owners' decision, reserve records, unconfirmed payments, annulment when no payment exists); rules and approved amounts stay frozen.
7. **Owner distribution recording** — **New product decision**: an explicit decision record with its effect (03 §5 asked explicit record vs generic money movement). How and when the amount is paid out, and whether it is a money movement in Dinheiro, is not designed.
8. **Retained / reinvested amount** (01 §2 `Decided` concept; 03 §5 recording open) — for the post-Slice-06 gap-closure pass (§19). Not designed and no new transaction invented; no Fecho screen depends on such a record. It may overlap with the contextual-rule decision (a person lowering their period rule is already supported, but the amount never appears as "retained"); an explicit record that reduces what is paid but not what was earned is the other candidate. The canvas carries this as a sticky note.
9. **Advances above earned at close** — clamp `Decided` (04 J5); policy `Open` (03 §5 · Advance policy). The person's payable is 0 and never negative; earned value is unchanged; the excess is shown as its own case and its own position term (money that left the business), never as earned remuneration, debt or credit, and never netted against anyone. Whether it is carried over, recovered, written off/absorbed or treated otherwise is a management decision this slice does not design.
10. **Reserve capture and balance** — pre-implementation design gap for the post-Slice-06 gap-closure pass (§19). Reserve allocation is a canonical money movement (03 §2 #8) but no slice captures it, and spending from the reserve is not capturable (an expense has no "paid from reserve" mark). Fecho reviews the period's allocation as a record captured elsewhere, keeps it distinct from expenses and out of the free amount, and shows no running balance. No capture flow was invented here.
11. **Cash check** (C1; 03 §5). Candidate only. If adopted: the expected-cash formula (services, advances, expenses and team payments in cash; purchases cannot enter until Slice 03 records a method), whether a difference becomes a home exception, and whether it should ever block closing.
12. **Contribution treatment at close** (03 §5): still à parte, shown as a fact and never as an exception, prerequisite or deduction; the pilot may expect netting.
13. **Period model** (03 §5): the period chip reuses Slice 04's sheet; period length and the period switch inside Fecho are not designed.
14. **Corrections as exceptions** (04 J5 candidate): not shown because no correction flow exists.
15. **Hoje entry**: Slice 01's attention row deep-links to the Fecho home; which Fecho states should surface on Hoje, and to whom, is open (05 §4).

## 13. Assumptions

Sample data only; currency is an organization setting. One rule per person per period at a time (a change replaces it with a record). Contextual rules are percentages of the person's own production. Approval is period-level and all-at-once. The first confirmed payment moves the period to *Em pagamento*. A closed period's summary is derived exactly as when it was open. Attributions name whoever confirmed within the authorized context. Nothing in Fecho writes to Hoje's shared *Recentes*.

Formal accounting, P&L, tax, payroll documents or payslips; owner accounting; reporting beyond the position; desktop layouts; notifications; messaging; auth mechanics; correction flows; deletion; cash-drawer or POS management; multi-period comparisons; per-service consumption; configuration of periods, rules or categories; reserve-allocation capture.

## 18. Canonical tensions found and decisions this slice asks for

- 04 J5 step 4 says "resolve exceptional remuneration/contribution cases"; for contribution cases there is nothing to resolve in V1 (03 §5 keeps settlement open), so the design only restates them at approval. If the pilot expects netting, J5 and 03 §2 #9 need a decision.
- 03 §2 #12 names a "basic operational financial overview"; the design's *Livre para decisão dos sócios* is a specific reading of it (production minus earnings, costs, reserve and any amount delivered above earned, each as its own term). It should be validated with the pilot as the number they actually use when deciding.
- 01 §2 lists the retained/reinvested amount as a distinct `Decided` concept, but no journey or slice captures it; either it is given a representation (Q8) or 03 §5 should record that V1 does not represent it.
- 03 §2 #8 requires reserve allocations as money movements, but no slice captures them; Fecho can only display what another space records (Q10).
- 04 J5 lists corrections as a candidate exception while no slice has a correction flow; the exception cannot exist before the flow does.
- **Decided in the correction pass** (03 §2 · Period states; 04 J5): the four states, confirmed payments before *Fechado*, close blocked while approved amounts are unpaid.
- **Decided in the final semantic correction** (04 J5): remaining payable per person = `max(earned − advances − confirmed payments, 0)`; never negative; an excess advance is not converted into earned remuneration, debt or credit and stays a separate management case. The policy for the excess (carry-over, recovery, write-off/absorption, other) stays `Open` (03 §5 · Advance policy).
- **Design choice, not canonical:** subtracting the excess from *Livre* as its own term follows from "the money already left the business" (Sollelio brief); *Livre* itself is a Slice 06 reading (see above).
- **New product decisions** this slice still introduces, none of which contradicts a `Decided` item and all of which 04 J5 and 03 §5 must take explicitly: the journey steps as hypothesis; period-level all-at-once approval; a rule changeable until approval, then only by annulling the approval before any payment; partial payments warned and recorded, over-payments blocked; close allowed without an owners' decision (warn only); mandatory reopen reason and the reopen target state; the explicit owners'-decision record; the circularity stance in §6 (two independent records, no derivation, any order, negatives warned not blocked).

## 19. Pre-implementation design gaps (post-Slice-06 gap-closure pass)

Small, separate pass before architecture and implementation planning; neither item blocks the Fecho UI as designed.

1. **Reserve allocation and use.** Canon requires reserve allocation as a money movement distinct from an expense (03 §2 #8, 04 J3) and keeps reserve money in the business and out of distribution (01 §3). No slice captures an allocation or a use of the reserve (Slice 03 excludes it; an expense has no "paid from reserve" mark). Fecho only reviews recorded movements. To design: where and how an allocation and a use are captured, who confirms them, and whether a running balance is wanted.
2. **Retained / reinvested amount.** A `Decided` money concept (01 §2) with no product representation (03 §5). It may overlap with the contextual-rule decision (lowering a period rule) or need its own record that reduces what is paid but not what was earned. To decide: which representation, whether it is a Fecho decision or an Equipa fact, and how it appears in the person's situation and the position. No transaction was invented in Slice 06.

Also still open and not part of this gap-closure pass: the policy for an advance above earned value (03 §5 · Advance policy: carry-over, recovery, write-off/absorption, other), and partial payments, overpayments, first-payment authorization timing, payment correction and the reopen target state (03 §5 · Payments within the close). Slice 06 shows these cases without deciding them.
